Math Detective

Every math question is a case. Use the same four moves each time.

1. Read itWhat is asked? What is given?
2. Plan itWhich tool fits?
3. Solve itWork one step at a time.
4. Check itDoes the answer make sense?

Fractions: what’s left over?

Case: A garden is planted with beans, peas and carrots. 1/3 is beans. 1/4 is peas. What fraction is carrots?

Read it: The whole garden is 1. Carrots are whatever beans and peas don’t cover. Plan it: Use equal parts so the fractions can be added.

Press the button to start.

Your turn

A pizza is 1/2 cheese and 1/6 mushroom. The rest is olives. What fraction is olives?
Hint: 2 and 6 both fit into 6. Write 1/2 as sixths first.
A cake is 1/4 chocolate and 1/2 vanilla. The rest is strawberry. What fraction is strawberry?
Hint: Write 1/2 as 2/4. Add the two parts, then subtract from 4/4.
A field is 2/5 corn and 1/10 wheat. The rest is grass. What fraction is grass?
Hint: Write 2/5 as tenths first.

Percent off: find the sale price

Case: A $40 jacket is 25% off. How much do you pay?

Read it: “Percent” means out of 100. “Off” means take away. The question asks for the sale price, not the discount. Plan it: Find the discount, then subtract it. Or skip a step: you pay 100% − 25% = 75% of the price.

Green is what you pay. Red is the discount.

Your turn

A shirt costs $60. It is 15% off. What is the sale price?
Hint: 15% of 60 is 9. Is $9 the discount or the sale price?
A game costs $80. It is 20% off. Which calculation gives the sale price in one step?
Hint: You pay 100% − 20% = 80% = 0.80 of the price.
A bag costs $50. It is 10% off. What is the sale price?
Hint: 10% of 50 is 5. Is that the discount or the sale price?
A hat costs $20. It is 50% off. What do you pay?
Hint: 50% means half.

kg and grams: move the decimal point

Case: How many grams are equal to 4.6 kg?

Read it: The prefix kilo means 1000. So 1 kg = 1000 g. Plan it: Going from kg to g, multiply by 1000. Going from g to kg, divide by 1000.

Find the angle k

Case: A figure shows one angle and the letter k. Find k.

Read it: Look for clues in the figure. A small square means 90°. A straight line means 180°. Three sides means a triangle (180° inside). Plan it: Pick the rule, then subtract what you know from the total.

Your turn

Two angles sit side by side on a straight line. One is 38°. The other is k. What is k?
Hint: A straight line is 180°. Subtract 38°.
A triangle has angles of 50°, 70° and k. What is k?
Hint: Angles in a triangle add to 180°. Add 50 + 70 first, then subtract from 180.
Two angles make a square corner (90 degrees). One is 35 degrees. The other is k. What is k?
Hint: A square corner is 90 degrees. Subtract 35.
An angle of 115 degrees and an angle k sit on a straight line. What is k?
Hint: A straight line is 180 degrees. Subtract 115.

Equivalent fractions and mixed numbers

Case: Which mixed number is equivalent to 7/4?

Read it: Equivalent means the same amount written differently. Plan it: Divide the top by the bottom. The whole number is how many full wholes you can make. The remainder is what is left over.

Your turn

Which mixed number is equivalent to 9/4?
Hint: 9 ÷ 4 = 2 remainder 1. Keep the same denominator.
Which fraction is equivalent to 3/5?
Hint: Whatever you do to the top, do to the bottom.
Which mixed number is equivalent to 7/3?
Hint: 7 divided by 3 is 2 remainder 1.
Which fraction is equivalent to 2/3?
Hint: Do the same to the top and the bottom.

Patterns: tables, rules and graphs

Case: A pattern starts at 3 and adds the same amount each time. Which term number has a given value?

Read it: What is the first term? What is added each time? Plan it: Work backwards. Subtract the first term, divide by the amount added, then add 1.

Your turn

A pattern is 2, 6, 10, 14. What term number has the value 26?
Hint: It adds 4 each time. (26 − 2) ÷ 4 = 6 jumps. Then add 1.

Hint: Test each rule on position 2. It must give 10.
A pattern is 5, 8, 11, 14. What term number has the value 29?
Hint: It adds 3 each time. (29 - 5) / 3 = 8 jumps. Then add 1.
Position 1 has 3 tiles, position 2 has 6, position 3 has 9. Which rule fits?
Hint: Test each rule on position 2. It must give 6.

  1. Find the two tens your number sits between.

  3. 5 or more? Go up. 4 or less? Stay down.
